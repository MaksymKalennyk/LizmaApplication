Privacy Policy

Last updated: {{lastUpdated}}

Lizma is a social app for sharing music with your friends. It is operated by {{operator}} ("we", "us"). This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. Questions: {{contactEmail}}.

What we collect

  • Account: your username, your password (stored only as a one-way hash) and when you accepted our Terms.
  • Profile: if you add them, a display name and a profile photo. We keep the photo as a new square image without the camera, date or location details the original file may carry.
  • What you create: posts (the track, its cover, its 30-second preview and Apple Music link, and your caption), comments, reactions and comment likes.
  • Your connections: friends, friend requests, invite links you create and users you block.
  • Notifications: if you allow them, the push token Apple gives your phone for Lizma, and what you chose to be notified about (Profile → Notifications).
  • Reports you send about posts, comments or users.
  • Feedback you send from the app (Profile → Send feedback): your message, its topic, the app and iOS version, and, only if you enter it, an email address so we can answer you.
  • Technical data: our server logs IP addresses and request times to keep the service secure.
  • On your device: the app keeps your sign-in token in the iOS Keychain and a few settings in app storage. They stay on your device.

How we use it

  • To run Lizma: show your posts to your friends, build your feed, find people, and send you the notifications you chose (about friend requests, reactions, comments and a daily reminder to share a song).
  • To keep the community safe: review reports, remove content that breaks our Terms and suspend abusive accounts.
  • To improve Lizma and answer you: we read your feedback and, if you left an email address, reply to it. We use that address for nothing else.
  • We do not sell your data, show ads or track you across other apps and websites.

Who can see your data

  • Your posts, comments and reactions are visible to your friends. Your username, display name and profile photo can be seen by other users who find you or see your comments, unless you block them.
  • When you search for music, your search text goes from your phone to Apple's iTunes Search service, without your account details; covers and previews also load from Apple. Opening a track in Apple Music or Spotify hands over to that app or website. Apple's and Spotify's own privacy policies apply to them.
  • Notifications are delivered by Apple Push Notification service: we send Apple your phone's push token and the notification (for example who commented and the start of the comment).
  • Companies that host our servers process data on our behalf. New reports and feedback are also sent by email to our support mailbox, which Google (Gmail) hosts for us.
  • We may disclose data when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account (in the app: Profile → Delete account), we permanently delete your profile (with its name and photo), posts, comments, reactions, likes, friends and requests, invite links, notification settings and push tokens, the reports you sent and your feedback (emails that already reached our support mailbox are deleted by us on request). Server logs are kept for a limited time for security.

A push token is also removed when you sign out on that phone, or when Apple tells us Lizma is no longer installed there.

If we suspend an account for breaking our Terms, we remove what it posted and its connections and keep only the account record (username and password hash) and the related reports, so the account can't simply be opened again. If you can't sign in and want that record deleted too, email us.

Your rights

You can see and change your content in the app, delete your account at any time, and contact us to ask for a copy of your data or to exercise any other rights you have under the law where you live (for example the GDPR).

Children

Lizma is not meant for children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Passwords are hashed, and access to your data requires your sign-in token. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
